# epidesc 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* Provides `epiyearweek()` to format dates in epiyearweek (`"yyyyww"`) format.
* Provides the epidemiological descriptors `desc_Ap()`, `desc_Tp()`,
  `desc_Cnf()`, `desc_Cmax()`, `desc_Cmed()`, `desc_Isof()`, `desc_p()`,
  `desc_Cwf()`, `desc_Cwmax()`, `desc_Cwmed()` and `desc_Inc()`.
* Provides `desc_list()` with the catalogue of available descriptors and
  `desc_year()` to compute them per spatial unit and epidemiological year.
* Includes the `dengueRio` example dataset.
